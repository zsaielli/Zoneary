Security

Report a vulnerability.

Pre-launch · updated 2026

Security is core to what we build. This is our pre-launch disclosure process; a formal security policy and advisory channel will follow as products ship.

Responsible disclosure

Please report security issues responsibly by emailing info@zoneary.com with as much detail as possible, before disclosing them publicly. We'll acknowledge your report and work with you on a fix. A dedicated security mailbox will follow as products ship.

What helps us

  • A clear description of the issue and where you found it.
  • Steps to reproduce, and the impact you believe it has.
  • The product, version, and environment where you observed it.

Please avoid

  • Accessing or modifying data that isn't yours.
  • Testing against systems or deployments you don't own or control.
  • Publicly disclosing details before we've had a chance to respond.

Our approach

Zoneary products are local-first by design — they run on your own hardware and keep operational data under your control. We treat honesty about system state as a security feature: when something is unknown, we say so rather than showing a comforting green.
