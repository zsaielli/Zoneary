The process

Four steps. One beautiful upgrade.

No ripping walls. No new drilling. Sentinel works with what you already have — and what you get looks like nothing you've seen on a wall before.

01

Your wiring stays. Exactly as-is.

Every door sensor, window contact, motion detector, siren and buzzer you already own stays connected. Reuse the sensors and wiring already in your walls — without replacing the entire system.

02

A Konnected board bridges the old panel.

Add a Konnected board alongside your existing alarm panel — it links all your sensors and wiring onto your local network, where Sentinel can read them. No cloud dependency, no monthly fees.

03

Install Sentinel on hardware you own.

Run Sentinel on a computer you already have — a Raspberry Pi, a mini PC, even a spare laptop. A guided wizard sets it up to run quietly in the background, 24/7. No internet or cloud required.

04

Mount your display. Access on your network.

Mount any tablet where the old keypad was — instant mission control. Access Sentinel from any browser on your local network, with secure remote access available through supported user-controlled methods.
