Privacy

Privacy notice.

Pre-launch draft · updated 2026

This is a pre-launch privacy notice for the Zoneary website. A full, finalized privacy policy will be published before any product is sold. It will be updated as our products and infrastructure are finalized.

What this website collects

The Zoneary marketing website is a static site. It does not run analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not ask you to create an account. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, whose delivery network may receive your browser's request as part of serving those files.

Information you send us

If you email us or submit the early-access form, we receive the details you choose to send — such as your name, email address, organization, and product interest — so we can reply and keep you informed. We use it only for that purpose and do not sell it.

Our local-first commitment

Zoneary products are designed to run on your own hardware and network. Operational data — video, telemetry, and security-system state — is intended to stay under your control, without mandatory cloud dependency. Product-specific data practices will be documented with each product at launch.

Questions about privacy? Email info@zoneary.com.
