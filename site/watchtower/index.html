Why Watchtower exists

Video management split in two. Neither half was for us.

Watchtower is the missing middle. Professional capability without institutional arrogance — built for the small installation and the large one, on the same software, with the same honesty.

A real VMS should be judged on one night: the night you need the footage. Watchtower is built for that night — and honest with you every night before it.

Questions

The honest answers.

What people ask before they trust a VMS with the one night it has to work.

Does Watchtower need an internet connection?

No. Watchtower runs on your own hardware, and live view, recording and playback are designed to work on your local network with the uplink down. Internet access is for the things that genuinely need it, not for watching your own cameras.

Where is my footage stored?

On storage you chose, in the building you chose. Core local operation does not require cloud video storage, and does not upload your recordings to Zoneary. Sharing a clip is an action you take, not a default you have to opt out of.

Is there a per-camera fee?

No — Watchtower does not charge per camera. Adding a camera is not a licence purchase. The wider commercial structure is not final; editions and ownership terms will be published before launch.

What are the commercial terms?

Only one is settled today: Watchtower does not charge per camera. The direction we're building to is software you own rather than rent — but final editions, ownership terms, update options and support terms have not been decided, and we won't publish them until they are.

Which cameras work with Watchtower?

Watchtower uses ONVIF-based setup and profile discovery with standards-based RTSP video ingestion, rather than a per-brand integration list. Automatic local-network camera discovery is still in development.

Hardware used during development and interoperability testing currently includes i-PRO, MOBOTIX Q71 and MOBOTIX MOVE / MOVE U2VC-series equipment. That is a test bench, not a supported-device list. Compatibility details will be published before release; until then, ask us about a specific model and we'll tell you what we know.

How does Watchtower relate to PulseGrid and Sentinel?

Watchtower manages the video. PulseGrid monitors the infrastructure behind it — servers, storage, network, power and environment. Sentinel modernizes and controls the home security system. Each stands on its own; together they're the Zoneary ecosystem.

Can I buy Watchtower today?

Not yet — Watchtower is pre-launch. Request early access and we'll bring you in as it rolls out, along with editions and pricing when they're published.
