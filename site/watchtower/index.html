Questions

The honest answers.

What people ask before they trust a VMS with the one night it has to work.

Does Watchtower need an internet connection?

No. Watchtower runs entirely on your own hardware — live view, recording and playback all work on your local network with the uplink down. Internet access is for the things that genuinely need it, not for watching your own cameras.

Where is my footage stored?

On storage you chose, in the building you chose. Nothing is uploaded to a Zoneary cloud, and there is no remote service holding a key to your recordings. Sharing a clip is an action you take, not a default you have to opt out of.

Is there a per-camera fee?

No. Adding a camera doesn't trigger a purchase order. Watchtower is licensed per system, not per lens — the same software runs a four-camera house and a two-hundred-camera operation.

What happens if I stop paying?

The version you bought keeps running. Update coverage is optional: if it lapses, you stop receiving new releases — you don't stop recording. A cancelled invoice can't disable what you already own.

Which cameras work with Watchtower?

Watchtower is built on open standards — ONVIF for setup and profile discovery, RTSP for video ingestion — rather than a per-brand integration list, so most modern IP cameras are candidates.

Our development and interoperability testing currently runs on i-PRO, MOBOTIX Q71 and MOBOTIX MOVE / MOVE U2VC-series hardware. That's what we've actually verified, not a universal compatibility guarantee. A published compatibility list is on the roadmap; until then, ask us about a specific model and we'll tell you what we know.

How does Watchtower relate to PulseGrid and Sentinel?

Watchtower manages the video. PulseGrid monitors the infrastructure behind it — servers, storage, network, power and environment. Sentinel modernizes and controls the home security system. Each stands on its own; together they're the Zoneary ecosystem.
